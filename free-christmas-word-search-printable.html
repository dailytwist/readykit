How to use it

Print the first page for each child and keep the answer page for yourself. Kids circle each word in the grid and tick it off the list below. Words go across, down and diagonally, but never backwards, so younger readers can find them without getting frustrated. It takes about ten minutes, which makes it a good quiet activity while dinner is in the oven, on a long car trip, or at the end of a classroom party.
