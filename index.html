Questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

Is anything shipped to me?

No. Everything is a digital download, so you get it instantly and there is no shipping cost.

Do I need Microsoft Office?

No. The spreadsheets work in free Google Sheets, and the resumes open in free Google Docs. PDFs open in any PDF app.

Can I use the planners on an iPad or tablet?

Yes. The planners are hyperlinked: tap a month tab or a date to jump to it in GoodNotes, Notability, Xodo or any PDF app.

Which paper size do I get?

Both: US Letter and A4 versions are included where it matters.

Can I use the files for my business or clients?

Personal purchases are for you and your household. For a company, school, team or client work, choose the Business License bundle. See licenses.
